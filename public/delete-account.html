Delete Your Account

You can delete your DripMaxx account directly inside the app:
  • Go to Profile
  • Scroll down
  • Tap Delete Account
If you cannot access the app, you may request deletion by contacting:

mangaficustomercare@gmail.com
When your account is deleted:
  • Your profile and data(which includes pictures you have uploaded) will be permanently removed
  • Your scan history will be deleted
  • This action cannot be undone
