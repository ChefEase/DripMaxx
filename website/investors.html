Company summary

Personal style guidance, on demand.

DripMaxx is an independently founded technology startup headquartered in Saint John, New Brunswick, Canada. The Android product is publicly available on Google Play, with its iOS and App Store release coming soon.

The problem

Useful fashion advice is difficult to access.

Most people do not have affordable, immediate access to personalized fashion guidance. Inspiration platforms show what others wear, but rarely explain how a user's own colors, fit, proportions, trend relevance, and preferences work together.

The solution

Scan, understand, improve.

DripMaxx turns an outfit photo into evidence-based scores and practical next steps. Users can follow their progress, join challenges, compare looks, and build confidence over time.

Mission

Make personalized, practical style guidance instantly accessible.

DripMaxx is being built as a global AI styling and fashion-discovery platform where people can improve their style, participate in communities, discover creators and brands, and make more confident clothing decisions.

The product

More than an outfit score.

A cross-platform consumer product combining personalized AI analysis, progress tracking, competition, and rewards.

01

Granular AI analysis

Independent scoring for color, fit, trend, personal style, and body compatibility, supported by category-level criteria.

02

Actionable recommendations

AI-generated suggestions explain what works and how a user can strengthen their outfit.

03

Personal progress

Scan history, outfit comparison, rankings, and rewards turn feedback into an ongoing style journey.

04

Social engagement

Country and style leaderboards, private groups, challenges, community voting, XP, and scan rewards.

Market and go-to-market

Built for a social, style-conscious generation.

The initial audience and launch strategy below reflect the founder's current direction and will evolve as DripMaxx gathers more user data.

01

Primary audience

Gen Z users ages 16–24 who are fashion-conscious and interested in self-improvement, fitness, dating, streetwear, confidence, and social media.

02

Secondary audience

Women seeking accessible outfit feedback, practical styling advice, and greater confidence in what they wear.

03

Market rollout

Canada is the launch market. The United States, United Kingdom, and Australia are the next markets under consideration.

04

Direct competitors

Current competitive research includes ULook, Twirl, Rate Outfit, Plan Your Outfit, and FitCheck.

05

Broader alternatives

Users may also turn to ChatGPT for outfit advice, Pinterest for inspiration, Instagram, TikTok, and fashion creators.

06

Indirect competitors

Adjacent AI self-improvement products include Rizz AI, Umax, and Cal AI.

07

Creator strategy

Start with micro-creators across fashion, gym, streetwear, sneakers, hair, college, and dating content.

08

Growth channels

Prioritize organic TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Reddit, and micro-influencer content, with minimal paid advertising initially.

09

Brand partnerships

Brand deals and retail partnerships are not currently part of the plan and will remain open for future consideration.

Founder story

Onyia Kamsiyochukwu Alexander

Founder · 19 · Saint John, New Brunswick, Canada

Onyia is a Software Engineering student at the University of New Brunswick and the founder of DripMaxx.

He created DripMaxx to help people improve their confidence through fashion. Too many people second-guess what they wear before going out, while useful fashion advice can be expensive, inconsistent, or inaccessible. DripMaxx gives people instant AI-powered outfit feedback so they can become more confident in how they dress.

Onyia built DripMaxx from idea to public launch—designing its backend, building its mobile experience, integrating its AI pipeline and payments, and publishing the product on Google Play while continuing his university studies.

The journey required learning mobile development, building and calibrating an AI pipeline, navigating Play Store publishing, integrating payments, and preparing the backend to scale.

“Confidence starts before you speak—it starts with how you feel about yourself. Our goal at DripMaxx is to help millions of people feel more confident every time they get dressed.”
